        In 2016, my girlfriend and I went on a six-month road trip through Australia,
        completing the iconic "Big Lap" around the coast in our trusty Mazda Tribute "Chuck". On the way there, we stopped
        over in Delhi to visit the incredible Taj Mahal. Starting and ending in Sydney, our journey took us to the Great Ocean Road,
        the beautiful Karijini National Park, and the challenging Gibb River Road, including the Pentecost River crossing.
        We also visited Uluru, did our Open Water diving course at the Great Barrier Reef, and saw so much of this incredible
        continent along the way. On the way home, we stopped in Bali to dive the amazing reefs there.
