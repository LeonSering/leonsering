        In 2018, my girlfriend and I traveled through Namibia and Botswana, starting in Windhoek with a typical white
        Toyota Hilux and rooftop tent. We visited Fish River Canyon and the stunning dunes of Sossusvlei. The highlight

        to the breathtaking Victoria Falls and ended the trip with a mokoro ride through the Okavango Delta.
